Did you know?

The name Roblox is a blend of two words: robots and blocks.

Before it was Roblox, the project was called DynaBlocks.

Welcome to Roblox Rewind!

[Picture coming soon]

Today Roblox is one of the biggest gaming platforms in the world, but it started out much smaller. In the mid 2000s it was a simple physics sandbox where players stacked bricks, built their own places, and fought in blocky sword battles.

This site takes a look back at that early era, from the first DynaBlocks demos in 2004 to the classic games, avatars, and currencies of 2006 through 2008.

What you will find here

  • The Beginnings: how two founders turned a physics idea into Roblox
  • Timeline: the key dates from 2004 to 2008
  • Classic Games: Crossroads, Rocket Arena, and other early favorites
  • Avatars & Tix: hats, Builders Club, Tix, and Robux
  • Gallery: pictures and a classic Bloxy Cola sound
  • Contact: share your own old school Roblox memories
