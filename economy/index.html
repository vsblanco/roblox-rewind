Goodbye, Tix

Tix stuck around for almost ten years. They were removed on April 14, 2016, leaving Robux as the only currency.

Avatars & Tix

[Picture coming soon]

Early Roblox avatars were made of six simple parts: a head, a torso, two arms, and two legs. Players could change their body colors, faces, and hats, and later on they could wear shirts and pants made by other players.

Two currencies

Tickets (Tix) were the free currency. Players earned them just by playing, such as logging in each day, and could spend them on hats and other items in the catalog.

Robux replaced the original Roblox Points in 2007. Robux were the premium currency, bought with real money or handed out every day to Builders Club members.

Builders Club

Released in August 2007, Builders Club was a paid membership for players who wanted more out of Roblox.

Daily Robux
Members received a Robux allowance every day.
Clothing
Members could create and sell their own shirts and pants.
More places
Members could own more places than free players.
Later on
Builders Club was replaced by Roblox Premium in 2019.
