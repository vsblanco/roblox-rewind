BrickBattle tools

Most classic fighting maps gave players the same set of gear:

  • Sword
  • Rocket launcher
  • Slingshot
  • Superball
  • Time bomb
  • Trowel (builds a wall)

Classic Games

Before obbies and simulators took over, the most popular places on Roblox were BrickBattle maps. Players spawned with a set of tools and fought to knock each other's towers, and each other, apart.

Early favorites

Crossroads (2006)

Built by John Shedletsky, Crossroads was the most popular Roblox place of 2006. Players battled across towers, bridges, and floating islands, and almost everything could be blown up.

Rocket Arena

One of the early 2006 places, Rocket Arena was a fast free for all where players fired rockets at each other inside a walled arena.

Other places to remember

  • Sword Fights on the Heights: sword battles on high platforms where falling off was just as dangerous as your opponent.
  • Chaos Canyon: a free for all BrickBattle shooter from around 2006 and 2007.
  • Glass Houses: a fighting map where the buildings could be shattered.
  • Work at a Pizza Place: a 2008 role play game where players took orders, made pizzas, and delivered them.
