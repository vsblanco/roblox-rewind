The Beginnings

Roblox was created by David Baszucki and Erik Cassel. Years earlier, the two had worked together on Interactive Physics, an educational program that let students build and test simple physics experiments on a computer. Watching kids use that software to build their own creations gave them the idea for a much bigger playground.

From DynaBlocks to Roblox

In 2004 Baszucki and Cassel began testing the first demos of their platform under the name DynaBlocks. In 2005 the project was renamed Roblox, and it spent that year in beta testing while the team worked on the physics engine and the building tools.

From idea to launch

  1. Baszucki and Cassel build educational physics software together.
  2. They begin testing DynaBlocks demos in 2004.
  3. The project is renamed Roblox in 2005 and enters beta.
  4. Players start building and sharing their own places.
  5. Roblox officially launches on September 1, 2006.

What it was like

Early Roblox looked very different from today. Avatars were made of simple blocks, every world had studded bricks, and most games were either building places or BrickBattle maps where players fought with swords, rockets, and slingshots. Players built their places with Roblox Studio and could add behavior to their bricks with Lua scripts.
