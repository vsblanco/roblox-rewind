Quick facts

  • Founders: David Baszucki and Erik Cassel
  • First name: DynaBlocks
  • Launch: September 1, 2006

Timeline: 2004 to 2008

Here are some of the biggest moments from the early years of Roblox.

Early Roblox milestones
Date Milestone
2004 David Baszucki and Erik Cassel begin testing early demos under the name DynaBlocks.
2005 The project is renamed Roblox and goes through beta testing.
2006 Crossroads, a BrickBattle map built by John Shedletsky, becomes the most popular place of the year.
Sept. 1, 2006 Roblox officially launches to the public on Windows.
March 2007 Safe chat is added for players under 13 to follow COPPA, a children's online privacy law.
2007 Robux replaces the original Roblox Points and is used alongside Tickets (Tix).
August 2007 Builders Club, a paid premium membership, is released.
2008 Work at a Pizza Place is released and becomes one of the longest running Roblox games.
Later on, Tix were removed on April 14, 2016, and Builders Club became Roblox Premium in 2019.
